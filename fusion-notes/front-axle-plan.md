## Design principle (from the user, 2026-11-01)

**Place each part through a dependency chain of the real geometry that defines it, the way it's fabricated. Do not use measured distances from the center or the tube end.**
- Example: axle center → Inner C (inside face, `inner_c_gap`) → kingpin axis (Ø1" hole) ∩ shaft axis → U-joint center.
- Only use a number where the fabricator actually measures or sets one (gap, caster, mount spacing), or where it's a dimension of the part itself.
- This gives maximum flexibility: change a defining feature and everything downstream follows.

## Goal

Everything on the axle is positioned from a **declared axle center** (`axle_center`). The center is never derived from the tube ends. Build order follows the real build:

1. axle center
2. Inner C positions
3. tube cut to length from the Inner Cs.
